Expert-Dominant exponent (γ=11.4) amplifies the performance gap, assigning disproportionately higher weight to the best classifier rather than treating all models as equal.

▶
2
OAM
Outlier-Aware Modification
Asymmetric
if |p_i − μ| > δ: RC_i × k
δ (disagree limit)0.21
HIGH outlier k (lenient)0.30
LOW outlier k (aggressive)0.10

Asymmetric by design — a lone fracture signal (HIGH: p > μ) is penalised leniently, preserving sensitivity. A lone no-fracture dissenter (LOW: p < μ) against fracture consensus is suppressed aggressively. Missing a fracture is clinically costlier than a false alarm.

~19% trigger rate · val set
